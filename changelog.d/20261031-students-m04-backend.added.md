Add M04 Students (backend): the student record (profile, identity number encrypted and masked, programme, batch, section, roll number, term), created automatically when an admission is confirmed, added by hand, or imported from CSV. Guardians, enrollment status changes with history (leave, detained, year back, suspended, graduated, dropped out, transferred out; leaving removes the Student role), student documents with verification, and student self-service (own record, contact edits, document upload, correction requests that staff approve). Heads of department and faculty see only their department's students. Staff onboarding now grants the Faculty/Staff role as a system action, so HR officers do not need every faculty permission. API notes in `docs/api/modules/M04-students.md`.
